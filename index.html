What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers pretend to be trusted organizations or individuals to steal sensitive information like usernames, passwords, banking details, OTPs, or personal information.

Victims are usually tricked into clicking fake links, downloading malicious attachments, or entering confidential information on fake websites.

Identity Theft

Personal information can be stolen and misused.

Financial Fraud

Attackers may steal banking credentials.

Account Compromise

Hackers gain unauthorized access to accounts.

Types of Phishing Attacks

Phishing attacks come in different forms. Understanding each type helps you identify threats before they cause harm.

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to be from trusted organizations asking users to click malicious links or provide personal information.

Smishing

Fraudulent SMS messages that trick users into opening fake websites or downloading malicious applications.

Vishing

Voice phishing where attackers impersonate banks or organizations over phone calls to steal confidential information.

Spear Phishing

Highly targeted phishing attacks aimed at a specific person or organization using personalized information.

Warning Signs of Phishing

Recognizing these warning signs can prevent your personal information from being stolen.

Unknown Sender

Emails from unfamiliar or suspicious senders.

Fake Links

URLs that imitate trusted websites with slight changes.

Urgent Messages

"Act Now!" or "Your account will be blocked!" messages.

Unexpected Attachments

Files that may contain malware or ransomware.

Poor Grammar

Many phishing emails contain spelling and grammar mistakes.

OTP or Password Request

No legitimate company asks for passwords or OTPs by email.

Example of a Phishing Email

Examine the email below and identify the suspicious elements.

From: support@paypa1-security.com

Subject: Your Account Has Been Suspended

Dear Customer,

We detected unusual activity in your account. Your account will be permanently suspended unless you verify your details immediately.

Click the link below to verify your account:

https://paypa1-security-login.com

Failure to respond within 24 hours will result in account closure.

Thank You,

Real Website vs Fake Website

Always verify website URLs before entering sensitive information.

Legitimate Website

https://www.amazon.com

  • HTTPS Secure Connection
  • Correct Domain Name
  • Valid SSL Certificate
  • Professional Design

Fake Website

https://amaz0n-login.xyz

  • Misspelled Domain
  • Unknown Extension
  • Requests Personal Information
  • Suspicious Login Page

How to Stay Safe Online

Follow these cybersecurity best practices to protect yourself from phishing attacks and online scams.

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords using letters, numbers, and special characters.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security with OTP or authentication apps.

Verify Website URLs

Always check the website address before entering login credentials.

Don't Open Suspicious Emails

Ignore emails requesting passwords, OTPs, or financial information.

Avoid Unknown Downloads

Download files only from trusted and official sources.

Keep Software Updated

Install updates regularly to patch security vulnerabilities.
